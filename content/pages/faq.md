  - heading: Planning and reserving your stay.
    text: |
      **How do I check availability and book?**

      Choose a villa and select its Check availability action. You will then continue to the existing external booking partner to see live dates, rates and complete the reservation.

      **Can I complete my booking on this website?**

      No. This website helps you compare the villas, but the reservation is completed securely on the external booking platform.

      **Does this website take online payments?**

      No payment is processed on this website. Any card or payment steps required for your reservation will be shown by the external booking partner.

      **Where can I see current rates?**

      Rates depend on the villa and travel dates. Use the booking link for your chosen property to see current availability and pricing.

      **What is the cancellation policy?**

      Read the booking policy summary, then review the exact terms presented by the booking partner before confirming. The terms shown during booking take priority.
  - heading: Villas, locations and amenities.
    text: |
      **Where are the villas located?**

      Best E Villas has accommodation in Prospect and St. Silas on the West Coast and Providence on the South Coast.

      **Which amenities are included?**

      Amenities vary. Current property descriptions include combinations of air-conditioning, ceiling fans, Wi-Fi, cable TV, laundry facilities, equipped kitchens and private balconies. Confirm the exact list on the individual villa and booking pages.

      **Does every villa have access to a pool?**

      Facilities differ by location, so pool access should not be assumed for every villa. Check the selected property details before booking.

      **Is housekeeping included?**

      Current property information refers to weekday housekeeping for some stays. Confirm the schedule and what is included on the booking listing for your specific villa.

      **Can I request a crib or ask about a longer stay?**

      A crib may be requested in advance, subject to confirmation. For extended stays or larger family and group plans, contact Best E Villas before booking.
