  - heading: Information we may receive
    text: |
      This website does not provide customer accounts, an enquiry form or on-site payment. If you contact Best E Villas by telephone or email, we receive the information you choose to provide so that we can respond and help with your stay.

      Our hosting and website-security providers may process limited technical information, such as an IP address, browser or device type, requested pages, time of access and security events. This information is used to deliver, protect and troubleshoot the website.
  - heading: External booking and social services
    text: |
      Selecting **Check availability** takes you to the external Direct-book booking platform. Reservation details, payment-card information and any cookies used during that booking journey are handled by the external provider under its own privacy and cookie information.

      Links to our Facebook and Instagram pages also take you to third-party services. Their privacy terms apply once you leave this website.
  - heading: Cookies and analytics
    text: |
      Best E Villas does not currently activate analytics or advertising cookies on this website. The site is prepared for optional analytics, but no analytics service loads unless it is configured and the appropriate consent controls are in place.

      Hosting and security services may use strictly necessary cookies or similar mechanisms to deliver and protect the site. If optional analytics or marketing tools are introduced, this notice and the available cookie choices will be updated before those tools are activated.
  - heading: How information is used and shared
    text: |
      Information is used only as reasonably needed to answer enquiries, administer a guest relationship, protect the website, meet legal obligations and maintain the service. Best E Villas does not sell personal information.

      Information may be shared with service providers that support hosting, security, communications or bookings, and with authorities when legally required. Those providers receive only the information needed for their role.
  - heading: Retention, security and your choices
    text: |
      Personal information is kept only for as long as needed for the reason it was collected, for legitimate business records or to meet legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used, although no internet service can promise absolute security.

      Depending on the law that applies to you, you may ask to access, correct or delete personal information, object to certain uses, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact Best E Villas using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.
  - heading: Contact and updates
    text: |
      Questions about this notice or a privacy request can be sent to errolbest@bestevillas.com or discussed by telephone at +1 246 233 2814.

      This notice may be updated when the website, service providers or legal requirements change. The current version is dated 24 September 2026.
