      The current Best E Villas policy states that a valid credit card is needed to confirm a reservation. Booking takes place through the existing external platform, where the property, dates and final terms are presented before confirmation.

      - Read the booking partner's full terms before submitting the reservation.

    text: |
      The current website states that payment in full is due on arrival. This preview website does not collect or process payments. Follow the payment instructions supplied with your booking confirmation, as the final booking terms may be updated by the property or external platform.
  - heading: Cancellation
    text: |
      The policy currently published by Best E Villas states:

      - Cancellation is free when made more than 30 days before arrival.
      - For a cancellation within 30 days of arrival, full payment becomes due.

      Always use the terms attached to your own reservation when deciding whether to amend or cancel, and contact the booking provider if anything is unclear.
