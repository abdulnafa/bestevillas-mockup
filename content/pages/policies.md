  - heading: Confirming a booking
    text: |
      A valid credit card is required to secure every reservation. Booking takes place through the external platform, where the property, dates and final terms are presented before confirmation.

      - Check that the villa, guest count and stay dates are correct.
      - Read the booking partner's full terms before submitting the reservation.
      - Keep the confirmation issued by the booking partner for your records.
  - heading: Payment
    text: |
      Rates quoted by Best E Villas are per apartment, per night, with taxes and fees included. Cash and credit cards are accepted, and the remaining balance is paid in full on arrival.

      This website does not collect or process payments. Follow the payment instructions supplied with your booking confirmation.
  - heading: Cancellation
    text: |
      - Cancellation is free when made at least 30 full days before check-in. A cancellation made exactly 30 full days before check-in qualifies for free cancellation.
      - When fewer than 30 full days remain before check-in, the full booking amount is charged.

      Always use the terms attached to your own reservation when deciding whether to amend or cancel, and contact the booking provider if anything is unclear.
  - heading: Arrival and stay
    text: |
      - **Check-in:** 3:00 PM
      - **Check-out:** 11:00 AM
      - **Guests:** Guests of all ages are welcome.
      - **Pets:** Pets are not allowed.
  - heading: External booking platform
    text: |
      Selecting Check availability takes you away from this website to the existing booking partner. Availability, rates, reservation data and any required card details are handled there rather than on the Best E Villas website.
  - heading: Property-specific details
    text: |
      Amenities, occupancy, included services and house arrangements can vary by villa. Review both the individual villa page and its live booking listing before confirming your stay.
