Our story

Family-run in Barbados

Best E Villas is owned and operated by Errol Best. The aim is simple: provide well-equipped, convenient accommodation together with the personal attention of a local, family-run business.

Guests can choose between properties in Prospect and St. Silas on the West Coast or Providence on the South Coast, with options for different group sizes and travel plans.

The Best E Villas approach

Everything you need, clearly presented.

The villas are designed as practical bases for a Barbados stay, with useful home comforts and local support. Exact facilities vary by property and are listed on each villa page.

Comfortable stays

Furnished living spaces, equipped kitchens and everyday amenities give guests room to settle in.

Helpful locations

West and South Coast neighbourhoods offer a choice of settings for beaches, dining and island outings.

Local assistance

The family-run team can help with practical stay questions and local information before and during a visit.

Space to settle in

Made for everyday island living.

Current villa amenities include combinations of air-conditioning, Wi-Fi, laundry facilities, private balconies and equipped kitchens. Some locations also include shared outdoor facilities.
