Two coasts. Three welcoming neighbourhoods.

Stay close to the places you want to explore, with villas in Prospect and St. Silas on the West Coast and Providence on the South Coast.

West Coast

Prospect

Prospect sits on a peaceful ridge above Barbados’ West Coast. The location offers a convenient base for nearby beaches, restaurants and visits to the University of the West Indies, Cave Hill Campus.

  • Stay optionsTwo-bedroom and three-bedroom units
  • SettingResidential neighbourhood above the West Coast
  • NearbyBeaches, restaurants and the Cave Hill campus

West Coast

St. Silas

St. Silas Heights is an established residential neighbourhood with access from Highway 2A. It is well positioned for Warrens and West Coast outings, while Apes Hill and Royal Westmoreland are nearby for golf visitors.

  • Stay optionThree-bedroom units
  • SettingQuiet, established neighbourhood
  • NearbyWarrens, golf courses and West Coast beaches
View St. Silas villas

South Coast

Providence

Providence is set in a quiet neighbourhood on a ridge above the South Coast. Barbados Golf Club, Miami Beach, Oistins and St. Lawrence Gap are all within the wider area for days by the sea and evenings out.

  • Stay optionTwo-bedroom units
  • SettingResidential ridge above the South Coast
  • NearbyGolf, beaches, Oistins and St. Lawrence Gap
